    Projekt Terg has started, and Vixen arrived in a week

    Terg exists as a project in two streams. The first stream, Vixen, is a set of small tools that share one account and one look: notes, ideas, requirements, a dictionary, documents, compliance, keys and codes, architecture. The ninth is Eva-B, the first piece of the second stream: an open source autonomous core system for small businesses and solo proprietors.

    What is unusual is the time. A week ago there was a specification and a pair of hands; today they are live, tested, and readable — written in the open at github.com/vixenarchi/vpl. They are not natural. They have no face, no body, and they are one hundred per cent artificial. That is the point, and it is the start of the second stream rather than the end of the first.
