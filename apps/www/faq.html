questions

What people ask first

Do I need an account for each application?

No. One account works in all eight. Sign in at any address, then open another one and you are already in. Names and addresses ignore case; passwords never do.

What does it cost?

Nothing to start. The suite runs on the free tiers of a CDN and a database. The parts that use a model need a key — yours, from any provider — or credits bought at vp, where one credit is one call whatever its size.

Where is my data, and how do I get it out?

In one database, reachable only by you. Every note, requirement, control and document is markdown, and every app can copy the current text to your clipboard. Deleting your account deletes your rows; there is no archive you have to ask us for.

What happens to my model key?

It stays in your browser's own storage and is sent only to the provider you chose. You can also save it to your account, once, so the other applications pick it up — that is a drawer, not a proxy. If you would rather the key never live anywhere but a server, use vp credits instead: then no key of yours exists at all.

Does the model write my documents?

It proposes. Nothing it writes lands in a field on its own: it appears in a speech balloon, and a person presses use it or drop. What is written is yours, not the model's — and the one thing a model cannot give you is a fact it did not have.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. Every app is a list of cards on a phone and a two-column desk on a larger screen, and nothing scrolls sideways. Measured, not claimed: the suite's own tests open each app at 390×844 and check.

Can I share something with somebody who has no account?

Each app can make a read-only link to one thing. Some allow an editor. A shared link is a capability, not a login: whoever holds it can read that one document and nothing else. You can take it back at any time.

Why eight applications and not one?

Because a tool that does one thing can be read in a minute, replaced in a day and left alone for a year. One big application with eight screens grows a permission model, a settings page the size of a manual, and a reason for every team to be on it. Eight small ones need one account and no meetings.

Who is behind this?

Terg, and a person who answers the mail at info@terg.hr. The legal particulars are on the imprint page.
