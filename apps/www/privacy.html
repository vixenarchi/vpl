privacy

Short, because there is little to say

This page is the whole policy. If a sentence here turns out to be false, that is a bug and we would like to hear about it.

This website

No cookies. No analytics. No fonts, scripts or images loaded from anybody else. The only thing it stores is your choice of theme, in your own browser's storage, under the key vpl.theme. The server keeps whatever a web server keeps to serve a page and to stop abuse; we do not build profiles from it and there is no third-party code that could.

The applications

To use an app you sign in. We store your e-mail address, a password you chose (hashed by the auth service, never by us in the clear) and whatever you write. Your writing lives in one database and is readable only by you and by the people you explicitly share it with. Passwords are never case-insensitive and never logged.

Deleting your account deletes your notes, ideas, requirements, words, documents, controls, keys and architecture jobs. There is no copy to ask for afterwards, and no export we have to run.

Model calls

When you use a part of the suite that talks to a model, the text you are working with is sent from your browser to the provider whose key you are using, in order to get an answer. We do not see that request unless you use the shared door at api.terg.hr or vp credits — in which case we see the request itself but not who else you are.

If you save a key to your account, it is stored the same way your documents are: readable only by you. Keys issued by vp are shown once and stored only as a hash, so we cannot reprint yours even if you ask.

What we never do

  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anybody for any purpose.
  • No training of our own models on your writing.
  • No cross-app profiling: the applications do not share your content with each other, only your name.

Your rights

You can read everything you have written, copy it out and delete it, all from inside the applications, at any time. For anything else — a correction, a complaint, a question about where the database is — write to info@terg.hr and a person will answer.
