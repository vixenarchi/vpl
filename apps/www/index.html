Philosophy

Two ways of working, named after the two characters who carry them. Vixen and Eva are the avatars and the protagonists of the story and of the approach; the applications are Vixen's, and Eva is the direction.

v Vixen

Light, fast, and done before lunch.

  • Small applications, one purpose each, no layers to maintain.
  • Zero architecture: no graph, no metamodel, no dashboard that grows a dashboard.
  • Built for speed, creativity and fast changes of mind.
  • Short engagements — or none at all. Open the tool, use it, leave.
  • Wants everything now, for one precise purpose.

e Eva

The same lightness, kept for years.

  • Starts light, around a skeleton: the core is small and does not change.
  • Evolutionary architecture — layered, and the layers arrive when they are needed.
  • The system evolves in interaction with the person using it, on a no-code principle.
  • Evolutionary virtual agents take that work over as it grows.
  • Continuous engagement, and governance for larger systems.

Process

Three steps, whichever of the two you are doing.

  1. 1

    Use / learn / ideate

    Open an application and put something real into it. Half an hour in vn and vi is enough to know whether this way of working suits you.

About

TERG is a consulting practice for evolutionary systems and their architecture, built on the Vixen and Eva principles: solve the small problem now, keep the core small enough that it can still be changed in five years.

Everything here is available, or will be, on GitHub — the code, the decisions, and the reasons for them.
