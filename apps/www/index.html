terg · two streams, Vixen and Eva

Natty or not, here they come.

Self organized headless zero architecture zero deployment productivity assets in cloud. An open source autonomous core system for small businesses and solo proprietors.

They are not natural: no face, no body, one hundred per cent artificial. Notes, ideas, requirements, a dictionary, documents, compliance, keys, architecture — and a bookkeeper. Nothing is installed: everything is somebody's account, a browser, and a database in the EU.
