Natty or not,
here they come.

Self organized, headless, zero architecture and zero deployment productivity assets in cloud. Open source autonomous core system for small businesses and solo proprietors.

They are not natural: no face, no body, one hundred per cent artificial. Notes, ideas, requirements, a dictionary, documents, compliance, keys, architecture — and a bookkeeper.

Philosophy

Vixen

Light, fast, done before lunch. One purpose per thing, no graph, no metamodel, no dashboard that grows a dashboard. Short engagements: open it, use it, leave.

Eva

The same lightness, kept for years. A skeleton that does not change, layers that arrive when they are needed, agents that take the work over as it grows.

Process

  1. 1 Put something real into a tool. Half an hour tells you whether this way of working suits you.
  2. 2 Read the code at github.com/vixenarchi/vpl, or bring the problem and have it answered.
  3. 3 Keep it and let it evolve. Things are added; nothing is rewritten to make room.
