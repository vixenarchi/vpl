philosophy ▾

Two streams

Named after the two characters who carry them. They are avatars and protagonists, not products.

Vixen

Light, fast, and done before lunch. Small applications, one purpose each; no graph, no metamodel, no dashboard that grows a dashboard. Short engagements — open it, use it, leave.

Eva

The same lightness, kept for years. It starts around a skeleton that does not change, layers arrive when they are needed, and evolutionary agents take the work over as it grows.

process ▾

Three steps

Whichever of the two you are doing.

1 · use, learn, ideate

Open an application and put something real into it. Half an hour in vn and vi is enough to know whether this way of working suits you.

2 · explore, consult

Read how it is built and run it yourself — github.com/vixenarchi/vpl — or bring the problem here and have it answered.

3 · adopt and scale

Keep it, and let it evolve: applications added as they are needed, nothing rewritten to make room.

Nine

One account, one look, nine separate applications in two streams. Vixen is the fast one: eight tools, one purpose each, done before lunch. Eva is the long version: it starts small around a core and grows with the work. Nothing is shared between them but your name and the way they are drawn — a note does not become a requirement unless you copy it there yourself.

Vixen eight applications, one purpose each

  • vnnotes

    A tree of notes with a reader. The one everybody starts with.

    vn.terg.hr
  • viideas

    A wall of sticky notes, a funnel, and a place to park what you are not doing yet.

    vi.terg.hr
  • vrrequirements

    One sentence per requirement, how hard it is, and where it stands.

    vr.terg.hr
  • vzdictionary

    The words your project uses, defined once, so nobody argues about them twice.

    vz.terg.hr
  • vddocuments

    A pile in, a document out, and every version of it kept.

    vd.terg.hr
  • vccompliance

    A control list checked line by line against evidence — a draft an auditor signs, not a certificate.

    vc.terg.hr
  • vpkeys & codes

    One credit is one call, whatever its size. Codes and keys shown once, never again.

    vp.terg.hr
  • vaarchitecture

    The pile in, a box list out — as text, as a drawing, or handed to an agent that already has an account.

    va.terg.hr

Eva one, and it is growing

  • Eva Bbookkeeping

    Double-entry bookkeeping for a small business: receipts in, VAT, cash and accounts out. German, and already in use. The first thing that grew around a person instead of the other way round.

    b.terg.hr
