what it is, and why it is like this

Philosophy

Vixen: light, fast, done before lunch

One purpose per thing. No graph, no metamodel, no dashboard that grows a dashboard. A tool is opened, used, and left — and the leaving is part of the design, because a tool you cannot leave is not light. Short engagements: half an hour tells you whether this way of working suits you.

The eight are small on purpose. Each one answers a single question: what did I write, what did I think, what must be true, what does this word mean, what goes in the document, is the control covered, how much is left, what is the box. Nothing here is a platform and nothing wants to be the place you live.

Eva: the same lightness, kept for years

A skeleton that does not change, layers that arrive when they are needed, agents that take the work over as it grows. The point is not to be big; the point is to still be the same shape after two years of somebody's business going through it.

Eva-B, the bookkeeper, is the first piece of the long half: double-entry bookkeeping for a small business, in German, already in use, and the first thing that grew around a person instead of the other way round.

The house rules that are not negotiable

One account, one look, one database. A page that needs a script to render is slower than the product. No third-party request, no analytics beacon, no cookie banner, because there is no third party. A number of colours never carries the only signal. A refusal nobody can see is not a refusal. And a finished job is a record.
