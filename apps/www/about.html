About

A project, not a company

Terg is a project in two streams. Vixen is the fast half; Eva is the long half; both live in the open. There is no shop, no price list, no sales team, and nothing here is sold — which is why there is nothing to declare and no imprint to read.

Location is project-dependent: the desk is in Falkendorf, Landkreis Erlangen-Höchstadt, Mittelfranken, Bayern. The database is in the EU, and the applications are a browser away from wherever you are.

Contact

No form, no calendar link, no chat window, no chatbot. One address, read by a person, answered when there is something worth saying. That is deliberate: hard to get is the price of not being a funnel.

What is not here

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party script, no cookie banner, no newsletter, no account you did not ask for. If something is missing that you expected, it was left out on purpose and the reason is in the philosophy.
